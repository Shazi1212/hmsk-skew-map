The loop
Fifteen to twenty minutes, once a week

Open your broker's chain on one expiry rule for every name, no exceptions. Pull three numbers per name: ATM IV, put IV at your delta, call IV at the mirror delta. Add the 1M return and RVOL from any free screener, and the next earnings date so an event never masquerades as sentiment. Then press Save this week's snapshot: the level is structural, the change is the signal, and the change only exists if last week was saved. Six weeks in, the Δ column becomes the most honest thing on this page.
